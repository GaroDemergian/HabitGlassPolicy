Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new habit?

Tap the + button on the home screen, enter a name, pick an icon and color, choose your schedule (daily, weekdays, or every N days), and save. You can also set optional reminders.

How do streaks work?

Streaks count consecutive due dates that you complete. If your habit is scheduled for weekdays only, weekends won't break your streak. Miss a due day, and your streak resets to zero.

Reminders aren't showing up. What should I check?

First, ensure notifications are enabled in iOS Settings > Notifications > HabitGlass. Also verify that reminders are turned on for that specific habit in the app. Reminders only fire on days when your habit is due.

Can I use HabitGlass on iPad or Apple Watch?

HabitGlass is currently designed for iPhone only. iPad and Apple Watch support may be added in future updates.

Is my data backed up or synced?

HabitGlass stores all data locally on your device. It does not sync to the cloud or back up automatically. Your habits are included in your regular iPhone backups through iCloud or iTunes.

How do I delete a habit?

Open the habit detail screen, tap Edit, then scroll down and tap "Delete Habit". This will also delete all completion history for that habit.

What happens if I mark a habit complete by mistake?

Tap the completion button again to un-complete the habit. Your streak will recalculate automatically.
