Privacy Policy

Your habits stay on your device.

HabitGlass is an offline-first habit tracker for iPhone. It does not create accounts, collect personal data, run ads, use analytics SDKs, track users, or sell data.

  • ✓No account required
  • ✓No analytics, ads, or tracking
  • ✓Habit data is stored locally
  • ✓Local notifications only

Effective date: May 13, 2026

Information HabitGlass Stores

HabitGlass stores the habits you create, including habit names, notes, schedules, completion history, and reminder settings. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage APIs.

Information HabitGlass Collects

HabitGlass does not collect personal data from you. The app does not transmit your habit data to the developer, does not use third-party analytics, and does not include advertising or tracking SDKs.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, HabitGlass asks iOS for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. Notification permission can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Data Sharing

HabitGlass does not share your habit data with third parties. There are no third-party services in the app that receive your habit names, notes, schedules, completions, or reminder settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your habit data remains on your device until you delete it. Deleting a habit removes that habit and its history from the app. Deleting HabitGlass from your device removes the app's locally stored data from that device.

Children's Privacy

HabitGlass does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if HabitGlass changes how it handles data. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the HabitGlass project page at github.com/GaroDemergian/HabitGlassPolicy.
